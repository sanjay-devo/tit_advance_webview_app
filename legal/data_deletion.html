App accounts and information

The current app has no account registration, sign-in, or in-app deletion request feature. Its project code does not implement a user database or a process that sends profile data to an app backend. No account deletion is therefore available from inside the app.

Clear data stored on your device

  1. Open Android Settings and go to Apps (the menu wording can vary by device).
  2. Select TIT Advance, then Storage or Storage & cache.
  3. Choose Clear storage/data to remove locally stored app/WebView data, or Clear cache to remove temporary cached files only. Confirm the action if asked.

Clearing storage/data may reset the app and remove local settings. It does not delete information held by a website, Google Play, or another third-party service. Device menus and effects can vary.

Important: the in-app Clear button

The Settings screen currently displays a “Clear” button, but its implementation only changes the button text to “Cleared!” briefly. It does not clear local storage, cache, an account, or server-side data. Use the Android settings steps above instead.

Request deletion from a linked service

If you submitted information to an external service through an app link or embedded page, send the deletion request directly to that service using its published privacy or support contact. The app developer cannot delete records held by a third party. For an app-related privacy question, contact the developer through the verified contact details on the TIT Advance Google Play listing; no support email or phone number is configured in this project.

Include enough detail for the relevant provider to identify the request, such as the service name and approximate date. Follow that provider's identity-verification instructions, but do not send passwords or one-time codes. The project does not define a deletion-processing deadline; the service receiving the request will explain its process and timing.

Verification and legally required retention

A provider may ask for reasonable information to verify that a request concerns you before acting. Applicable law may require some records to be kept or may permit retention for legal claims, security, or compliance. The app project does not define a retention schedule or identify any specific record that must be retained; any such exception depends on the provider and applicable law.

Deleting the app from your device alone may not remove data held by an external service. Contact that service directly when you need its records deleted.
