Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes what can be determined from the TIT Advance WebView Edition (version 1.0) project and how to make a privacy request. The app provides learning screens and links to services hosted by other organisations.

Information collected

The app source does not include an account sign-in, an app-operated form for submitting personal information, analytics code, or an app backend that receives user records. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other profile information in the app screens.

When you load the app or follow an external link, the relevant hosting and service providers may receive routine connection and device/browser information (such as an IP address, request details, or technical logs) as part of delivering their services. Their collection and handling are governed by their own policies.

How information is used

Information handled by the app's hosting or linked providers may be used to deliver pages and resources, maintain service operations, and respond to requests you choose to send. The project does not configure an app analytics or advertising service.

Storage and retention

The app code does not implement an account database or a mechanism that stores submitted personal details. A device or browser may nevertheless retain ordinary web content, temporary cache, or settings under its own WebView/browser behavior. External services may keep logs or information you provide to them under their own retention rules.

The Settings screen currently displays an “Offline High-Speed Cache” status and a “Clear” control. The Clear control only changes its label temporarily; it does not implement deletion. Do not rely on it to erase data.

Sharing and third-party services

The app loads the Poppins font from Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS script from cdn.tailwindcss.com, and an app image from indiacybercafe.com. Its BT-101 viewer embeds a form page from b.indiacybercafe.com. The app also links to WhatsApp, Google Play, and titadvance.dukans.in. Those providers may receive technical request data or information you submit directly to them. Review their privacy policies before using their services.

The app does not include code that sells personal information or sends app profile data to advertising or analytics partners.

Permissions

The web app source contains no request for device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or location. A linked website or your operating system may separately request permission; review the prompt and the relevant provider before allowing it.

Security

Reasonable care should be taken when using any internet service, but no website, transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment details, or sensitive identity documents through general support channels.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly request personal information from children. It does not have an age gate or an in-app account registration flow. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of linked websites and avoid entering a child's personal information into third-party forms unless appropriate. If you believe a child has sent personal information to a service through an app link, contact that service using its published support channel.

Your privacy choices and rights

  • You can choose not to follow external links or provide information to third-party services.
  • You can manage website permissions and clear app/browser storage through your device settings; the exact options vary by device.
  • Depending on your location, applicable law may provide rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. The app has no account or user-data deletion system. For information held by a linked provider, contact that provider directly.
  • For app-related questions, use the developer contact route on the TIT Advance Google Play listing. The project does not publish a support email or phone number.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The updated date above indicates the latest revision of this page.

This page describes the current project implementation; it is not a statement that linked third-party services follow the same practices.
